Privacy Policy

This page explains how Studio Paper Kite handles information provided through this website.

Last updated: August 2026

1. About this Privacy Policy

Studio Paper Kite respects your privacy and is committed to handling personal information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use our website, how that information may be used, and the choices available to you.

Depending on how you interact with our website, we may receive information such as:

  • Information you voluntarily provide to us

3. How We Use Information

Information provided through the website may be used to:

We may use analytics and similar technologies in the future to understand how visitors use our website and to improve its performance and user experience.

Where third-party analytics, advertising or tracking services are introduced, this Privacy Policy may be updated accordingly.

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies where required for website functionality, analytics, advertising or performance measurement.

We may use third-party platforms or services to support website hosting, analytics, advertising, communications or other business functions.

These third parties may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies and terms.

When you submit an enquiry through our contact form, the information you provide may be transferred to your email application for sending to Studio Paper Kite.

Please avoid submitting passwords, payment details, government identification numbers or other highly sensitive information through the website enquiry form.

However, no method of transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

We may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to enquiries, provide services, maintain business records, meet legal obligations or resolve disputes.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable privacy laws, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information we hold about you.

Requests can be made using the contact details below.

Our website and services are intended for businesses and general audiences and are not specifically directed at children.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our services, website, technology or legal requirements.

The latest version will be published on this page.
