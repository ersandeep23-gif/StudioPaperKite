OUR APPROACH

Good digital work starts with understanding the business.

Studio Paper Kite was created around a simple idea: businesses shouldn't need to work with a different provider for every part of their digital presence.

A website, brand identity, advertising campaign and search strategy should work together rather than exist as disconnected pieces.

That's why we bring design, technology and digital marketing together under one roof.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

Simple principles behind every project.

01

Purpose before decoration.

Design should support a business objective. Looking good matters, but working well matters more.

02

Clarity beats complexity.

Websites and marketing should make it easy for customers to understand what you offer and what they should do next.

03

Consistency builds trust.

Your website, graphics, branding and advertising should feel like parts of the same business.

04

Digital should keep evolving.

Your online presence should be able to adapt as your business, customers and market change.
